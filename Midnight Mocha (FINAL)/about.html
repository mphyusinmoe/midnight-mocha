Corporate social Responsibility

Our Midnight Mocha strives to offer sustainable quality coffee products internationally.

Plastic Straw Initiatives

Plastic Straw Initiative – We have eliminated the use of non-biodegradable plastic straws to raise awareness about the harmful effects of plastic straws on our planet.

TU100 – Small Businesses in Rural Areas

TU100 – Small Business in Rural Areas – our cafe supports TU100, a student-led initiative in Myanmar assisting small businesses in rural areas. Products such as our ‘Rice Me’ riceberry bar help to fund this effort. In addition, we are partnering up with local non-profit organisations to distribute the ‘Rice Me’ riceberry bar for their nutrition and well-being. We aim to deepen our integration with the local Myanmar community further in future CSR efforts.
