Our Beans

Our coffee plants in Kalaw bloom every year during the October to February period. Thereafter, the coffee fruits are ready to be harvested about 8 to 12 months after blooming.
After selection, washing, drying, hulling,formulated in roasting temperatures of 225 to 230 degrees Celsius allowing the beans to reach their peak flavour.
This results in a mild acidity and a full body and aroma sensory experience, producing a strong, rich cup of coffee with a distinctive earthy flavour.

Peak Roasted

There is a science to roasting coffee. But creating and capturing perfect flavor is an art.
Our roasting team combines the exacting requirements of a chemist with the instinctive timing and technique of an artist, to produce our distinctive line of blends and single origins.

Blending

To satisfy our customers to the best of our ability, we has developed its own coffee blend
Our Premium Blend is a coffee flavour that is rich in an aromatic experience and caters for those who prefer their coffee a little lighter. Indulge in a full sensory awakening as you charge towards the day ahead.
