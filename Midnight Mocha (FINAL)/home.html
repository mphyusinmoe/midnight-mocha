COFFEE DICTIONARY

presented by MIDNIGHT MOCHA

Espresso

a type of strong black coffee made by
forcing steam through ground coffee beans.

Mocha

a type of good quality coffee made from a specific coffee bean called Coffee Arabica, and it was originally only grown in Mocha, Yemen.

Cappuccino

a type of coffee made with espresso and milk that has been frothed up with pressurized steam.

Latte

a type of coffee made with espresso and milk that has been frothed up with pressurized steam.

Americano

a drink of espresso coffee diluted with hot water.

Dalgona

a frothy coffee drink made by whipping together coffee powder, sugar and hot water, then adding this mixture .

Iced

chilled coffee, typically sweetened or flavoured and served over ice..

Black

coffee with nothing added – no cream, no milk, no sweetener.
